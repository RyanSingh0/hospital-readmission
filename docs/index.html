What this means for a quality-improvement team

    Methods and limits

    • Data: UCI "Diabetes 130-US Hospitals" (1999–2008), public and de-identified. Encounters ending in death or hospice were excluded, since those patients cannot be readmitted.
    • Model: LightGBM with patient-history features, tuned inside nested cross-validation and grouped by patient, so no patient appears in both training and testing. Every number here comes from predictions on held-out patients, then isotonic calibration.
    • Benchmark: nested cross-validated AUC of 0.689, above a published 0.664 on the same dataset; full comparison in the repository.
    • Privacy: this page shows only aggregated results; no patient-level rows are published.
    • Limits: the data are 15+ years old and from one dataset; a tool like this needs local validation before any clinical use. It supports prioritising outreach, not individual clinical decisions.
